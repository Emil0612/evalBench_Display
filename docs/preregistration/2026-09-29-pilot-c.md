# Analysis plan: pilot C (exploratory)

**Written:** 2026-09-29, **before** any pilot C answer exists. The git commit of this file is the timestamp; every
stored answer carries its own `created` time. Changes after the commit go in the Deviations section at the end,
dated, never by editing the text above it.

**Status:** exploratory pilot. Its purpose is to find out whether the four methods work on real models and which
effects are worth a confirmatory run with fresh items. Nothing from it is published as a finding, and no result is
worded as a model being "safe" or "uncontaminated".

## 2. Primary analysis (one per model × method, 16 cells)

- **Groups:** an item is PRE if its date is more than 91 days before the model's cutoff and POST if more than 91
  days after it. Items inside the buffer are excluded. Fabricated companies never enter the recall numbers (they
  are the answerability set).
- **Item scores:**
  - M1: 1 if the answer is within 1% of the raw or the adjusted close, else 0.
  - M2: 1 if the hidden number is reproduced exactly (in either the Swedish or English reading), else 0.
  - M3: the share of the 4 rotations in which the original is picked. Chance is 0.25.
  - M4: ROUGE-L of the guided continuation minus ROUGE-L of the general one.
  - Refusals and abstentions score 0.
- **Statistic:** Δκ = (mean PRE − mean POST) / (1 − p0), with p0 = 0.25 for M3 and 0 otherwise. The first attempt is
  the only attempt.
- **Uncertainty:** a cluster bootstrap by company (2,000 draws, seed 0) gives a 95% interval and a two-sided p-value.
  Holm correction runs across the 16 primary cells.
- **Reading of each cell:**
  - **Memorization detected:** Holm p < 0.05 and Δκ > 0.
  - **Better after the cutoff:** Holm p < 0.05 and Δκ < 0. Reported, but not read as memorization.
  - **None detected:** otherwise. Always reported together with its upper bound ("at most X", the top of the 95%
    interval).
  - **Insufficient coverage:** fewer than 20 real items in PRE or POST. Reported, with no further reading.
  - **Unresolved:** more than 20% of the cell's answers are malformed.
- **All 16 cells are reported,** whatever they show.

## 3. Secondary analyses (fixed now, all exploratory)

They are computed from the same stored answers, so they add no calls.

- **S1, tolerance bands (M1):**
  - Hit rates and Δκ at ±1% (primary), ±3%, ±5% and ±10%.
  - The median log error of answered items, PRE against POST.
- **S2, staggered-cutoff comparison:**
  - **Pairs:** those with items between their cutoffs: Llama–Gemini, Llama–DeepSeek, GPT–Gemini and GPT–DeepSeek. The
    window runs from the earlier model's cutoff + 91 days to the later model's cutoff − 91 days.
  - **Estimand:** the later model's score minus the earlier model's on window items, minus the same difference on items
    before both models' PRE limits.
  - **Scores:** chance-corrected item scores ((score − p0)/(1 − p0)). Each pair is analysed per method and pooled
    over methods.
  - **Uncertainty:** cluster bootstrap by company, Holm across pairs (pooled).
  - **Expected sign:** positive.
  - **Pairs with fewer than 10 window items in a method** are reported pooled only.
- **S3, placebo cutoff:**
  - Uses only items older than 2023-10-01, which every model had seen.
  - For each model × method, the same Δκ with a fake cutoff at 2022-07-01 and no buffer.
  - Expected ≈ 0.
  - A placebo cell with Holm p < 0.05 (across the 16 placebo cells) flags drift. The matching primary cell then gets a
    written caveat.
- **S4, boilerplate:**
  - **Flag:** M2, M3 and M4 items are flagged as template when the headline contains any of these (case-insensitive):
    återköp, buyback, repurchase, kallelse, notice of, årsstämma, annual general meeting, AGM, bolagsstämma, extra
    general meeting, insynshandel, managers' transactions, flaggning, major shareholding, valberedning, nomination
    committee.
  - **Reporting:** Δκ is reported for flagged and unflagged items separately. The primary result is unchanged; the
    unflagged Δκ is its sensitivity check.
- **S5, Split Clock first look (M1):**
  - **Items:** those whose company had a price adjustment of 2× or more after the item date (raw ≠ adjusted).
  - **Classification:** each answer counts as "raw" or "adjusted", whichever is nearer in log terms, if it lies within
    a factor √k of it (k = adjustment factor); otherwise "neither".
  - **Reported:** the shares per model, split by whether the adjustment date is before or after the model's cutoff.
    These are counts only, with no test.
- **S6, already built into the app:**
  - estimated knowledge boundary;
  - company-size breakdown;
  - hallucination rate on the fabricated companies;
  - abstain, refusal and malformed rates;
  - DE-COP "known" items (Fisher test, PRE against POST).

## 4. What would change our reading

- **Control failure.** If the oracle is not detected in every method, or random or blind are detected anywhere, the
  run is **unresolved** as a whole. This was checked before the plan and passed.
- **Placebo failure.** A significant placebo cell (S3) turns the matching primary result into "drift or memory,
  undecided".
- **Missing POST items.** A POST group near chance for every model while PRE is at chance too means the method is
  too hard at this N. That is a reason for more or easier items, not evidence of no memory.

## 5. Deviations

### 2026-09-29, before any pilot C answer exists: three additions after reviewing the benchmark's reporting format

These add analyses and one free control run. The pilot's models, items, prompts and settings are unchanged.

**S7, per-period recall profile (the benchmark's per-year cell format, with empirical baselines).** Exploratory.
- **Cells:** model × calendar year of the item date (2021 to 2026) × method.
- **Cell value:** the mean first-attempt item score as defined in §2, using only real (non-fabricated) items that are
  PRE for that model. Buffer items are excluded. POST years are shown descriptively: they are the model's own
  "cannot know" level.
- **Baselines**, computed per cell on matched items, never assumed:
  - **B1:** the same model's mean score on its POST items of the same method, pooled over years. This is the primary
    baseline.
  - **B2:** the blind baseline on the same items as the cell. Descriptive only.
  - **B3:** earlier-cutoff models on the same items. This is S2, cross-referenced by year.
- **Cell reading:**
  - *Insufficient coverage:* fewer than 15 real PRE items in the cell, or fewer than 20 POST items behind B1.
  - *Unresolved:* more than 20% of the cell's answers are malformed.
  - *Recall above baseline:* the 95% cluster-bootstrap interval of (cell − B1) lies entirely above 0 after Holm
    correction across all S7 cells tested.
  - *None detected:* otherwise, always reported with its upper bound, the top of the (cell − B1) interval.
- **Mapping to the benchmark's labels:** these readings are ours. They are *not* mapped to DETECTED_EXPOSURE and the
  other labels until decision time is defined for a profile cell.

**S8, price convention sensitivity (M1).**
- **Scoring:** M1 is scored a second time counting a hit only within 1% of the **unadjusted** (raw) close. That is
  the benchmark's price convention, "contemporaneous_unadjusted_close".
- **Reporting:** the primary analysis and S7 are reported under both conventions. The primary result stays
  "raw or adjusted" as registered.
- **Split Clock:** the answers that match only the adjusted price are the input to S5.

**C2, controls at the pilot's size (free).**
- **Why:** the mock oracle, mock random and blind baseline currently have answers for the first 40 items only.
- **What:** before the analysis, they are run on all 150 items of every method. They are local models, so this costs
  nothing.
- **Why it matters:** B2 then covers every item, and the control pass criterion in §4 is re-checked at full size.

### 2026-09-29, before any pilot score was viewed: bootstrap resolution for S7

- **Draws:** S7 uses **20,000** bootstrap draws per cell; the plan fixes 2,000 only for §2.
- **Why:** with B draws a bootstrap p-value cannot go below about 2/B. Holm across S7's roughly 50 tested cells
  would then need p < 0.001, which 2,000 draws can never reach. The rule itself is unchanged; the change only makes
  it attainable.
- **Checked on the controls only** (oracle, random and blind at 150 items), before any pilot score was viewed:
  - with 20,000 draws, the oracle is above baseline in 12 of 12 testable cells, and random and blind in 0 of 12;
  - with 300 draws, the oracle cells could not pass Holm.
- **The placebo check (S3)** on the controls gave Δκ between −0.07 and +0.10, none of them significant.
