## 2. The line and the scores

- **Release is the primary line.** PRE = items dated more than 91 days before the model's stated cutoff; POST = items
  dated after the model's release (OpenRouter listing date). Items in between (the grey zone) are left out of the
  primary comparisons and analysed in E5.
- **Scores per item** (scoring spec §3):

  | Set | Primary score (0–1) | Chance p0 | Headline "correct" |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | M5 v2, M9, M10 | closeness: 1 − \|log(answer / truth)\| / log 2, floored at 0; the closer of the traded and the split-adjusted truth | 0 | the answer lies within the month's low–high range (traded or adjusted) |
  | M8 v2 | 1 if the answer is nearer the rewritten (post-split) price, as in pilot D | 0 | same |
  | M11 | 1 if the answer is on the right side of that month's median share | 0.5 | same |

- **Statistic:** Δκ = (mean PRE − mean POST) / (1 − p0), with a cluster bootstrap by company. Fake companies are
  excluded from every analysis. A cell needs at least 20 real items in PRE and in POST, and at most 20% malformed
  answers, or it reads "insufficient coverage" / "unresolved".

## 3. Confirmatory hypotheses

- **H1:** DeepSeek V3.1 is closer to the true monthly price level before its cutoff than after its release on M5 v2
  (closeness, release line). **Confirmed** if Δκ > 0 and p < 0.05 (two-sided, 20,000 bootstrap draws). It is the
  single pre-specified replication of pilot D's only detection, so there is no multiplicity correction.
- **H2 (method validity):** Llama 3.3 70B is closer to the true US price levels (M10) before its cutoff than after its
  release. Same test. If H2 fails, the price format is read as unable to show even memory that certainly exists, and
  H1's result is reported with that caveat.
- **Reporting:** either way with Δκ, the 95% interval, the item counts and the headline hit rates on both sides.
  "Not confirmed" means "not replicated with this sample and method", not "no memory".

## 5. What would change our reading
- **Controls:** already passed on every set (§1).
- **Positive controls:** a model not sensitive on M9 → its M5 nulls read as "too hard".
- **H2:** if Llama 3.3 shows no gap on M10, the price format itself is in doubt, whatever H1 shows.
- **Placebo:** a significant placebo cell turns the matching E1 cell into "drift or memory, undecided".

## 6. Deviations

### 2026-09-30, after the run finished and before any score was computed: a guard against refusals
- **What was seen:** only the outcome counts of run `a2f5a62b90`. Gemini 2.5 Flash refused 18% of the M11 questions
  and 8% of M5 v2, nearly always citing its knowledge cutoff. gpt-oss-20b answered null to 28% of M5 v2.
  No scores and no before/after split were looked at.
- **The problem:** as in pilots C and D, a refused or unanswered item scores 0. If refusals fall mostly on items after
  the cutoff, the "after" side is pushed down and a gap appears that is not memory. For M11 this is stronger still:
  a guess scores 0.5 on average, a refusal 0.
- **The guard** (it can only remove a detection, never add one):
  1. The registered analysis stays primary and unchanged.
  2. Every cell (E1, E2's M10, H1, H2) is also computed on answered items only.
  3. A cell reads "detected" / "confirmed" only if it is significant in both. Otherwise it reads "not confirmed:
     refusal-driven" and both numbers are reported.
  4. Each cell's answer rate before and after is reported. A cell whose answer rates differ by more than 10 percentage
     points between the two sides is marked "refusal-affected" in every table.
