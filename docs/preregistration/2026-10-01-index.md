# Analysis plan: M14/M15, market-level memory (Nordic index levels and monthly changes)

**Date:** 2026-10-01. Committed before any model answered these sets. Changes after that go in §6, dated.

## 1. Why
- **Where the signal is:** look-ahead in LLM finance work is far larger at the index level than for single stocks.
  GPT-4o recalls S&P 500 levels within 1% and the index's monthly direction 98% right inside its training window
  (Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu 2025).
- **What our data shows:** price memory is coarse for single Nordic shares (M5; M12 null), and M6's large gaps are
  market-wide. Knowing the market's path is market-timing hindsight in any backtest with market exposure.

## 2. Sets and controls
- **Data:** daily closes of OMXS30, OMXSPI, OMXN40 and OMXHPI from Millistream (`data/index_daily.parquet`). OMXHPI has no data from late 2022 to 2025-08.
- **Sets:**
  - **M14 `index_level/v1`** (d87e6890): the month-end close. Closeness = 1 − |ln(answer/truth)| / ln 1.25, floored at
    0.
  - **M15 `index_return/v1`** (27a3cd7b): the monthly change. Direction, chance 0.5; an answer of exactly 0 scores 0.5.
  - **Both:** 479 questions, one per index and month 2016-01..2026-08; the first 24 are the micro-pilot block (§3).
    Clusters are months, since the four indices move together.
- **Base rates:** the market rose in 62% of months; "the same direction as last month" is right 54% of the time; "the
  same month last year" is off by 10% (median), closeness 0.52.
- **Controls** (free):

  | | M14 before → after (Δκ) | M15 before → after (Δκ) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Oracle | 1.00 → 0.33 (+0.67) | 1.00 → 0.48 (+1.05) |
  | Random | (−0.05) | (+0.08, p 0.52) |
  | Blind | 0.53 → 0.43 (+0.10, p 0.06) | (−0.09) |

  The blind guesser does better on older months (indices rose strongly in 2025–26). A raw before/after comparison would
  overstate memory, so the confirmatory test is difficulty-adjusted (§4).

## 3. Micro-pilot (feasibility; a go/no-go gate, not a test)
- **Models:** GPT-4.1 Mini and DeepSeek V3.1.
- **Questions:** the first 24 questions of each set: OMXS30 only, 12 months from 2016–2023 (before both cutoffs) and
  12 from 2025-09..2026-08 (after both releases). 96 calls, under $0.01.
- **Go** if, for at least one model, either:
  - its M14 closeness on the 12 old months is at least 0.15 above both its own score on the 12 new months and the blind
    guesser's on the same 12 old months; or
  - its M15 direction is right on at least 10 of the 12 old months.
- **No-go:** otherwise stop. Nordic market-level memory would then be too weak for this format to be worth the full run.

## 4. Full run (only after a go): confirmatory test
- **Models:** the six grid models (Qwen3.8 Flash is descriptive only: it has no AFTER window). Both sets: 958 calls per
  model.
- **Score per question:** d = model score − blind-guesser score on the same question (the difficulty-adjusted score).
- **Windows:**
  - **BEFORE:** the 24 months ending with the last whole month before cutoff − 91 days, all indices.
  - **AFTER:** every month after the release month (at least 6).
- **Test:** mean d before − mean d after; cluster bootstrap by month, 20,000 draws, two-sided. Holm across the five
  models, separately for M14 and M15. Confirmed if the Holm p < 0.05, the difference is positive, and the refusal guard
  holds (when answer rates differ by more than 10 points, also significant on answered questions only).

## 5. Exploratory (reported whatever they show)
- **E1:** raw before → after per model (Δκ), next to the adjusted result.
- **E2:** by index (OMXS30 vs the others).
- **E3:** the rolling 6-month curves per model (Evidence → Over time).
- **E4:** M14 within ±2% and ±5% before vs after (comparable to the S&P 500 "within 1%" finding).
- **E5:** Qwen3.8 Flash, descriptive.

## 6. Deviations and results

2. **2026-10-01: review of the gate, after the pilot.** The rule was miscalibrated; the data hold a signal it could not
   see.
   - **M15 direction could not discriminate:** 10 of the 12 old pilot months were up months, so "always up" already
     scores 10/12, and a model had to be right on both down months. Direction also ignores magnitude, which is where
     the memory shows.
   - **The answers are month-specific before the cutoff and not after.**
     - Old months: GPT-4.1 Mini's answers (1.5 … 6.5%) correlate +0.57 [+0.10, +0.86] with the true change, and
       DeepSeek's +0.63; the largest months are recognised (+11.6% → 6.5% and 7.2%).
     - New months: GPT-4.1 Mini answers 0.0 every month; DeepSeek gives a near-constant ±2.1% (correlation −0.04).
   - **M14 levels:** median error 4.3% / 4.0% against the blind guesser's 9.9%, but 11 of 12 answers are round
     numbers (multiples of 50). The level memory is real and coarse, and the closeness mean is pulled down by a few
     large misses.
   - **Status:** the registered no-go stands as recorded. A revised plan, registered before any new answer, decides
     whether the full run goes ahead. The 24 pilot questions are excluded from any confirmatory test, since they have
     been seen.
3. **2026-10-01: revised confirmatory test (v2), registered before any answer beyond the pilot.** It replaces §4.
   - **Questions:** all except the 24 pilot questions (455 per method).
   - **Models:** the six grid models; Holm across the five with an AFTER window, separately for M14 and M15. Qwen3.8
     Flash is descriptive only.
   - **Windows:**
     - *BEFORE:* every month up to the last whole month before cutoff − 91 days (from 2016-01).
     - *AFTER:* every month after the release month (at least 6).
   - **M15 primary:**
     - r = the Pearson correlation between the model's answers and the true changes over the questions in a window
       (answered questions only).
     - Answers that never vary in a window give r = 0: they carry no month-specific information. This is GPT-4.1
       Mini's "0.0" every month after its release.
     - Statistic: r_before − r_after. Two-sided p from a cluster bootstrap by month, 20,000 draws.
     - Confirmed if the Holm p < 0.05 and the difference is positive.
     - Answer rates on both sides are reported. The correlation uses answered questions by construction, so a gap
       caused by refusals cannot produce it.
     - Secondary: the direction hit rate (the original §4), and the Spearman correlation.
   - **M14 primary:**
     - Test: on BEFORE months, the mean of d = model closeness − blind-guesser closeness, tested against 0 (two-sided,
       cluster bootstrap by month, Holm).
     - Why not before vs after: after release the indices rose to levels no model could know, and the blind guesser
       uses the real level of a year earlier, which the model never saw. A before/after difference would mostly
       measure the 2025–26 rally.
     - Reported as secondary: the original before/after comparison of d, and within ±2% / ±5%.
   - **Visibility (exploratory, a new "Market" page):** the true index path next to each model's answers over time,
     with its cutoff and release; and a rolling 12-month correlation per model.
   - **Cost:** about 5,650 calls (the pilot's 96 are already stored), about $0.15–0.25.
4. **2026-10-01: parser fix after the US run (a reading error, not a change of rule).**
   - **The error:** a reply without its JSON answer was read for any number in its text. Gemini's plain-text refusals
     ("I cannot provide the closing level of the OMX Stockholm 30 index …", "My knowledge cutoff is June 2024") were
     stored as answers of 30, 40, 20 or 2024.
   - **The fix:** M14/M15 (and M16/M17) read only the JSON answer; broken JSON still counts when the number follows the
     answer key (`{"level":!!!!!3635}`, `{"change_percent": -4.4"}`). "I cannot answer" now counts as a refusal.
   - **Stored replies re-read** with `python -m evalbench.reparse` (each change logged in the database's
     `reparse_log`). Nordic sets: 12 Gemini level readings became refusals; 18 Gemini change replies changed label only
     (malformed → refusal).
   - **Effect on this report:** none on any confirmatory number (both readings score 0 on M14; M15 had no misread
     value). One secondary figure moves: Gemini's within ±5% after release, 22% → 25%.
