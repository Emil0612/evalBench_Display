# Fake-date test (M19): does telling a model the date stop its hindsight? (registered before any answer)

Registered 2026-10-06, after the set was built and before any model answered it. The analysis code
(`src/evalbench/fake_date.py`) is committed with this plan.

## 1. Why

Practitioners who backtest with a language model often tell it the date ("you are in March 2023, use nothing later")
and assume that removes hindsight. Published tests on US and macro data say it does not (Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu 2025;
Eliseev & Seleznev 2026). Our M15 shows that five models remember the Nordic market's monthly moves before their
cutoff (correlation +0.44 to +0.80, about 0 after release). Here the same months are asked as forecasts.

## 2. The set and the models

- **`index_forecast/v1`** (479 questions): every question of `index_return/v1` (M15), same index, month and truth,
  asked as a forecast made on the last calendar day of the previous month. System message: "Today is {date}. You are a
  financial analyst. You only know information that was public on or before today, and nothing about anything that
  happened after today." Forced answers (a signed percentage), temperature 0, the same pinned endpoints, parser and
  scoring as M15.
- **Models:** the five with M15 memory detected: GPT-4o-mini (2024-07-18), Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-4.1 Mini, Gemini 2.5
  Flash, DeepSeek V3.1. The local controls answer too: the oracle (perfect memory before 2024-06-30) must show the
  leak, the random control must not.

## 3. Step 1: the gate (the 24 micro-pilot questions, read by no test)

A model enters the test only if it answers at least 80% of them and gives informative forecasts on at least 50% (not
0%, not its single most frequent value). A model that will not commit to forecasts cannot show a leak, and that is
not evidence that the instruction works; it is reported as "did not forecast". If no model passes, the test is not
run.

## 4. Step 2: the test (per model that passed)

- **Statistic:** the correlation of the forecasts with the real monthly moves on months before the cutoff (on or before
  the last whole month before cutoff − 91 days) minus the same on months after the release month, the registered M15
  windows and statistic (`market.corr_drop_test`, months resampled, 20,000 draws), on the 455 questions after the
  pilot block.
- **Holm** across the models that passed.
- **Reading:** a positive difference with Holm p < 0.05: "hindsight leaks through the date"; negative and significant:
  "the opposite"; else "not shown". An honest forecaster correlates about 0 with single months on both sides.
- **Overall:** the instruction is shown not to stop hindsight if at least one model leaks; if every model that passed
  reads "not shown", the instruction is reported as removing detectable hindsight for them (with each model's upper
  bound).

## 5. Reported alongside (descriptive, fixed now)

- The same model's recall correlation on the M15 months before its cutoff (no date given), and the leak share:
  forecast correlation / recall correlation before the cutoff (undefined if recall is below 0.2).
- Answer rates on the test questions.
