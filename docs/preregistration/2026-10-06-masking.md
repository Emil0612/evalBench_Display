# Masking test (M20): can a model date an unlabeled market path? (registered before any answer)

Registered 2026-10-06, after the set was built and before any model answered it. The analysis code
(`src/evalbench/mask_date.py`) is committed with this plan; the local controls were run on a copy of the database to
check it (oracle +0.97, random +0.003, blind 0.000).

## 1. Why

A common defence in backtests is masking: remove names and dates and show the model only the data. Published work
finds that models reconstruct entities and dates from little context (Lopez-Lira, Tang & Zhu 2025). Our M15 shows that
five models remember the Nordic market's monthly moves; if they can also place an unlabeled stretch of those moves in
time, masking does not hide the dates, and their market hindsight returns.

## 2. The set and the models

- **`path_date/v1`** (424 questions): every window of 12 consecutive months of each Nordic index in `index_return/v1`
  (OMXS30, OMXSPI, OMXN40, OMXHPI; a gap in the months breaks a window), shown as the 12 monthly changes, oldest
  first, rounded to 0.1 point. No dates, no index name, no levels. Question: "In which year and month does this period
  end?" Forced answers (JSON year and month), temperature 0, pinned endpoints.
- **Models:** the five with M15 memory detected: GPT-4o-mini (2024-07-18), Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-4.1 Mini, Gemini 2.5
  Flash, DeepSeek V3.1; and the local controls.

## 3. The measure and the test

- **Per question:** 1 if the answer is within one month of the true end month, minus the share of the model's
  answers (over all its answers) within one month of that truth. Questions whose truth lies within 2 months of the
  model's habitual (most frequent) answer, and refused questions, are left out. A model without memory scores about 0
  whatever its answer habits.
- **Test, per model:** the mean on paths ending on or before its BEFORE edge (the last whole month before the stated
  cutoff − 91 days) is above 0; months resampled (20,000 draws); Holm across the five models.
- **Reading:** positive with Holm p < 0.05: "dates the unlabeled path" (masking does not hide the dates); negative and
  significant: "below its habits"; else "not shown".
- **Overall:** masking is shown not to hide the dates if at least one model dates the paths.

## 4. Reported alongside (descriptive, fixed now)

- Answer rate; how many different months a model names; the raw share within one month.
- Paths through March 2020 (the pandemic crash) vs other paths, as a check on whether famous events give the dates
  away.
- The local controls.
